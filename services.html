Professional Water Borehole Drilling

With over 20 years of experience, Karua Drilling delivers precision borehole drilling services using state-of-the-art equipment and multiple drilling methods to suit any geological condition.

Hard rock and overburden capability Clean, accurately aligned boreholes Built for farms, homes and commercial sites

DTH Drilling

Down-The-Hole percussion drilling for hard rock formations. Fast, efficient, and produces clean boreholes.

ODEX Drilling

Overburden drilling for unstable formations. Simultaneous casing advancement prevents collapse.

Mud Rotary

Ideal for unconsolidated formations. Fluid circulation stabilizes the borehole during drilling.
